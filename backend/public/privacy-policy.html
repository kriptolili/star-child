Privacy Policy for Star Child Tales

Last updated: August 11, 2026

Star Child Tales ("we", "our", "the app") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices, in accordance with Apple App Store requirements and applicable privacy laws, including protections for children's data (COPPA).

1. Information We Collect

  • Name – the parent/guardian's name and the child's first name, used to personalize stories.
  • Email Address – used for account creation, login, and customer support.
  • User Content – story preferences, birth date/zodiac sign, and other details you provide to generate personalized stories, illustrations, and audio.
  • Purchase History – subscription tier and transaction data, processed through Apple's In-App Purchase system.
  • User ID – an internal identifier used to associate your account with your generated content and subscription status.

2. How We Use Information

  • To generate personalized stories, illustrations, and audio narration.
  • To manage your account, subscription, and purchases.
  • To provide customer support.
  • To maintain and improve app functionality and reliability.

3. Children's Privacy

Star Child Tales is designed to be used by parents/guardians on behalf of their children. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children without parental involvement. Story content is entered and controlled by the parent/guardian at all times.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with service providers necessary to operate the app, including:

  • Cloud infrastructure providers (for hosting and storage of generated content)
  • AI service providers (used to generate story text, illustrations, and audio)
  • Apple, for processing in-app purchases

5. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active, or as needed to provide the service. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us.

6. Your Choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us at the email below.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or loss.
